
PIF/PUF is a narrative framework, born in 2014, that splits attention into:
- **PIF (Population Impact Factor)**: Tangible events judged by how many real people are affected (Cat 1–5).
- **PUF**: Narratives around the rest — Politics, Policies, Understanding, Fun, Forgotten Conflicts.
- **JBSM**: Not news — Jabber, Blabber, Sour, Misc.

**Status**: A U.S. provisional (#63/845,773, filed July 2025) expired without conversion. The work is MIT-licensed, unpaid, and not for sale. Credit to Pauline Gonen-Smith for the framework is the requested acknowledgment.

Examples: https://github.com/PAGSAdmin/PIF-PUF

---

## Why PIF/PUF Isn’t Just Another Tool

Most open-source AI projects fight misinformation with **detection** — bots, deepfakes, claims.  
PIF/PUF fights with **triage**.

> **How many real people are affected? (PIF — Population Impact Factor)**  
> **What kind of story is the rest? (PUF)**
